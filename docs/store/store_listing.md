## Full Description

### English

```
📏 DimCam - Dimension Marking Camera

Easily add precise measurement annotations to your photos! Perfect for:
• Construction & renovation projects
• Furniture measurements for online selling
• DIY and crafting documentation
• Real estate photography
• Product photography with dimensions

✨ KEY FEATURES

📷 Photo Management
• Capture photos directly or import from gallery
• Organize annotated photos in grid view
• Non-destructive editing - original photos preserved

📏 Precise Annotations
• Place start and end points with precision
• Enter exact measurements with multiple units (mm, cm, dm, m)
• Zoom and pan for accurate point placement

🎨 Customizable Styles
• 3 endpoint styles: T-Arrow-T, T-T, Arrow-Arrow
• 6 color options: Red, Green, Blue, White, Black, Purple
• Adjustable line width (1-10 pixels)

✏️ Flexible Editing
• Drag control points to adjust annotations
• Undo/Redo support
• Edit annotation properties anytime

💾 High-Quality Export
• Export annotated images at high resolution
• Configurable scale factor for text/lines
• Save directly to device gallery

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• 100% offline - no internet required
• No data collection or tracking
• No ads, no in-app purchases
• Open source (MIT License)

🌍 MULTI-LANGUAGE
• Full English and Chinese support
• Auto-detection based on device language

DimCam is free, open-source, and respects your privacy. Perfect for anyone who needs to document measurements on photos!

GitHub: https://github.com/zfdang/dimension-marking-camera
```
